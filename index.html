Common Screen Symptoms

  • Screen goes black randomly: This can sometimes be caused by a loose display cable or internal damage.
  • Flickering or flashing lines: This might indicate a problem with the graphics processor or the display connection.
  • Dim display even on full brightness: This could suggest an issue with the backlight or an internal setting malfunction.
  • Cracks or visible damage: Physical impact is often the cause, affecting the delicate screen components beneath the glass.

What You Can Do to Prevent Screen Issues

  • Always close your MacBook gently to avoid stress on the hinge.
  • Use a padded sleeve when carrying it in a backpack.
  • Keep liquids far from your laptop to avoid accidents.
